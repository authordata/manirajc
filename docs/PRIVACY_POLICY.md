# Privacy Policy for HearU

**Effective Date:** August 30, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to HearU ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the HearU mobile application (the "App").

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect the following types of information:
- **Personal Information:** Email address, phone number (used for OTP verification).
- **Communication Data:** Chat messages, friend requests, and mood ratings.
- **Usage Data:** Analytics regarding how you interact with the App.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information for various purposes:
- **Matching:** To connect you with appropriate peers for emotional support.
- **AI Responses:** To provide automated support via Gemini AI.
- **Analytics & Improvements:** To understand usage trends and improve our services.
- **Safety:** To detect and respond to potential crises or violations of our terms.

## 4. Data Security, Encryption, and Storage
Your data is stored securely. We implement industry-standard encryption for data at rest and in transit. Access to sensitive data is strictly limited to authorized personnel.

## 5. User Rights
Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Request deletion of your personal data.
- Export a copy of your personal data.

## 6. Third-Party Services
We may share your data with trusted third-party service providers, including:
- **Gemini AI:** For processing chat messages to provide AI-driven support.
- **Twilio:** For delivering SMS-based OTP verification.
These providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

## 7. Mental Health Data Protections
We recognize the sensitive nature of emotional support data. We apply enhanced security measures to protect this information and strictly prohibit its sale to third parties.

## 8. Data Retention
We retain personal information only for as long as necessary.
- **Chat Messages:** Automatically deleted after 90 days.
- **Account Data:** Retained until you request account deletion.

## 9. GDPR and CCPA Compliance
If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, you have specific rights under the GDPR or CCPA. Please contact us to exercise these rights.
